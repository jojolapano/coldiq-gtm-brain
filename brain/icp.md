# ICP — who we target in campaign `fr-es-gtm-hiring-v1`

Scoring rubric and tiers live in CLAUDE.md §4. This file explains the WHY.

## Accounts
- B2B SaaS or AI companies, 20–500 employees.
- GTM team in France or Spain first (other EU = secondary).
- Primary signal: an open "GTM Engineer" / "Growth Engineer" / "RevOps" role.
  Why: they are about to buy data + enrichment tooling, and the new hire will choose it.

## Not a fit
- < 10 employees (DIY / self-serve buyers).
- Data or enrichment providers.
- Known ColdIQ customers (see brain/offer.md).

## Buyers (in order)
1. Hiring manager of the open GTM role (Head of Growth, Head of RevOps, VP Sales).
2. CRO / VP Sales.
3. Founder (companies < 50 employees).

## Their likely pains (hypotheses to validate in learnings.md)
- Paying for several data tools with overlapping coverage.
- Engineering time spent on provider keys, rate limits and data normalization.
- New GTM hire needs results fast; stack decisions get made in the first weeks.
