# Voice — how copy sounds

## Language
- FR contact → French. ES contact → Spanish. Otherwise English.
- Formality (to A/B test, log results in learnings.md):
  FR: "vous" by default. ES: "tú" by default.

## Tone
- Peer to peer: one GTM operator writing to another.
- Specific over clever. Every sentence must be checkable.
- Short: max 90 words per email (CLAUDE.md §8).

## Always
- Open on the signal (their open role), stated as a fact with no flattery.
- Give value before asking: the 3 campaign ideas are the value.
- End with a soft question, never a meeting request in email 1.

## Never
- "I hope this finds you well", "revolutionise", "game-changer", "quick question".
- Fake urgency, fake familiarity, invented numbers.
- Any result not listed in brain/proof.md.
